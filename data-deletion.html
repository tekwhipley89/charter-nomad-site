CHARTER: Nomad

Data Deletion Instructions

How to Delete Your Data

CHARTER: Nomad stores all gameplay data locally on your device. If you have signed in with Google, a copy of your save is also stored in a hidden app-specific folder in your personal Google Drive.

You can permanently delete all of this data from within the app:

  1. Open CHARTER: Nomad
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Reset All Data
  4. Read the warning, then tap Confirm Reset

What Gets Deleted

This action permanently erases:

  • All game progress (Reach, heroes, missions, items, Eras, Fractures)
  • All settings and preferences
  • Your cloud save in Google Drive (if signed in)

What Is NOT Deleted

  • In-app purchases — These are managed by Google Play and tied to your Google account, not the app. Purchased cosmetic bundles and ad removal will be restored automatically via Google Play.
  • Your Google account connection — You can sign out separately from the Account section in Settings.

Alternative: Clear App Data

You can also delete all local data through your device's system settings:

  1. Go to Android Settings > Apps > CHARTER: Nomad
  2. Tap Storage > Clear Data

Note: This only clears local data. To also delete your cloud save, use the in-app Reset All Data option first.
